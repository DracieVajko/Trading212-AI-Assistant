## Ranking method (verbatim intent)

price/sector reaction, (ii) a plausible multi-session transmission mechanism,
(iii) three publicly listed corporate exposures, (iv) clear invalidation
criteria. "Continuation" is an editorial label, not a probability or return
estimate.

  stop and a defined invalidation.
- Peer read-through is conditional, never guaranteed.
- Sector rotation (AI → defence/industrials/value) is part of the thesis;
  position sizing and diversification matter more than a single call.
